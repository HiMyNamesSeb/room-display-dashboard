# Room Display Dashboard

📓 **Build blog:** [Blogs / room-display-dashboard](https://github.com/HiMyNamesSeb/Blogs/tree/main/room-display-dashboard)

## What I'm building and why

I'm building a cheap DIY wall-mounted screen for my room. It shows my Google Calendar across the top two-thirds, my Google Tasks in the bottom left, and a weather card in the bottom right, all over a slow pixel-art background like falling leaves or stars. Anything I add to Calendar or Tasks on my phone or PC should show up on the wall without me touching the display.

I want one place in my room where I can glance at my day without picking up my phone. I also want to build it myself on a tight budget, around $100, instead of buying a smart display. The rough idea is a Raspberry Pi Zero 2 W driving a thin portable monitor, plugged into the wall full time, showing a small web page in kiosk mode.

> **Transparency note:** I do the physical build, wiring and testing myself. **Claude (an AI assistant by Anthropic) helps me write the code** and also helps write and format this repo and the blog.

- **Goal:** Have every part on the checklist in hand for under $100 if possible, and no more than about $130.

**Estimated total: ~$103-112 before NC tax** (Day 1). Only the monitor and Pi prices are confirmed.
